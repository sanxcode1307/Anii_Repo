HOW IT STARTED

From a Simple Idea to a Special Place

Aniket Café started with a simple dream — to create a place where people could slow down, enjoy something delicious and spend time with the people who matter.

What began as a love for coffee and good food slowly became something bigger. A warm space, freshly prepared treats and an atmosphere that makes every guest feel welcome.

Today, every cup we serve and every dessert we create carries the same passion that inspired us from the very beginning.
